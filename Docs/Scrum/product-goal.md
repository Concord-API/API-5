# Product Goal

Let the user find out, in minutes and with auditable provenance, **how a legal topic is
being decided** in TJSP, TJRJ and TJMG, with an objective measure of how settled that
understanding is — instead of digging through one ruling at a time.

## Three commitments

- **The result is a topic, not a list of cases.** A search engine returns documents; Ratio returns the pattern.
- **Every number states its source, extraction date and scope.** The user cites what they read here, so a number without a basis is not information.
- **When the data does not exist, the product says so.** It never fills the gap with a plausible value.

## Who it is for

A single persona: the **user**. Ratio is a local application, running on the user's
intranet. The same person needs both things the product does — to *support* a position
(does the thesis hold, what is usually awarded, which ruling to cite) and to *decide*
consistently (what the court has already settled, whether panels diverge, whether a binding
precedent applies).
