# Sprint Backlog — Sprint 1

**Window:** Sep 7 to Sep 27, 2026

## Sprint goal

Stories the team commits to delivering. If any of them is not completed, the sprint did not meet its goal.

| ID | Priority | Description | SP |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| **US-01** | Must | As a **user**, I want to type my case's topic in natural language and get curated legal topics — not a list of cases — so that I can find out how it is being decided without digging through one ruling at a time | 8 |
| **US-02** | Must | As a **user**, I want results ordered by how settled the understanding is, and not by text relevance, so that I find first what supports my thesis | 2 |
| **US-06** | Must | As a **user**, I want a 0-to-100 score telling me how settled the understanding on the topic is, so that I know whether the thesis is worth arguing or is an open fight | 8 |
| **US-09** | Must | As a **user**, I want to read the topic's understanding in prose, opening with the number that answers the question and with the case count next to every percentage, so that I understand the pattern without opening a table | 8 |
| **US-21** | Must | As a **user**, I want to see the legal scholarship related to the topic, with author, work and a link to the article when there is one, so that I know what to cite beyond case law | 8 |
| **US-24** | Must | As a **user**, I want every screen to make clear that the data covers TJSP, TJRJ and TJMG, so that I do not draw a nationwide conclusion from a percentage that reflects three states | 2 |
| **US-25** | Must | As a **user**, I want to know the source and the extraction date of every number I am seeing, so that I know exactly what I am citing | 3 |
| **US-26** | Must | As a **user**, I want the screen to tell me what does not exist and why, instead of showing an empty field or a plausible value, so that I do not build a filing on data that does not exist | 3 |
